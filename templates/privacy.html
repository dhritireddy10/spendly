Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect the information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, and the expense records you log — including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to provide core features like expense tracking, spending summaries, and category breakdowns. We do not sell your personal data or use it for purposes beyond operating and improving Spendly.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely and is only accessible through your authenticated account. We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, or loss.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your personal financial data with third parties for marketing purposes. We may use trusted third-party services for infrastructure such as hosting, which handle data solely to support the operation of the app.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through the contact details provided on your account settings page.
